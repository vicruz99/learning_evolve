

To maximize the sum of radii for 26 circles in a unit square, we employ a force-directed optimization algorithm. This approach iteratively grows the circles while resolving overlaps through repulsive forces and boundary interactions.

### Strategy
1.  **Initialization:** Place 26 circles at random positions within the unit square with small initial radii to ensure validity.
2.  **Growth Phase:** Gradually increase the radii of all circles to push the system toward the maximum possible sum.
3.  **Repulsion & Boundary Resolution:** In each growth step, apply forces to resolve overlaps:
    *   **Pairwise Repulsion:** If circles overlap, a force pushes their centers apart proportional to the depth of the overlap.
    *   **Boundary Forces:** Forces push circles away from the four walls of the unit square to maintain boundary constraints.
4.  **Integration:** Update the centers based on calculated forces. If a circle gets stuck in a high-energy local optimum, it is perturbed to allow the algorithm to escape.

### Implementation
This code uses a high stiffness for boundary and pairwise forces to maintain strict geometric validity while a "shaking" mechanism prevents the packing from freezing in a suboptimal configuration.

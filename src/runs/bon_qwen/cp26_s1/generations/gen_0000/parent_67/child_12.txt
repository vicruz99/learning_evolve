3.  **Penalty Method**: Hard constraints (overlap and boundary violations) are converted into soft penalty terms in the objective function. This allows the optimizer to navigate the non-convex landscape more effectively.

    *   **Boundary Penalty**: If a circle is too close to the boundary, a penalty $(r_i - \text{dist\_to\_boundary})^2$ is applied.
4.  **Iterative Refinement**: To escape local optima, the solver runs the optimization from multiple random initial positions (restarted from the grid) and selects the best valid result.

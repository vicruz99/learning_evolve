A robust approach to finding a high-quality packing is to decouple the center positions from the radii optimization.
1.  **LP for Radii:** For any fixed set of 26 centers, the problem of finding the radii that maximize the sum of radii subject to non-overlap and boundary constraints is a Linear Programming (LP) problem. Specifically, we maximize $\sum r_i$ subject to $r_i \le \text{dist}(c_i, \text{boundary})$ and $r_i + r_j \le \|c_i - c_j\|$. This LP can be solved very efficiently.

We want to find center positions that maximize this score.

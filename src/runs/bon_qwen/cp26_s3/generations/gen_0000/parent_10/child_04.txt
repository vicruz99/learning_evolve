To solve this effectively:
1.  **Formulation**: We treat the centers $(x_i, y_i)$ and radii $r_i$ as optimization variables. The objective is to maximize $\sum r_i$, which is equivalent to minimizing $-\sum r_i$.
2.  **Constraints**:
    *   **Boundary**: Circles must be inside $[0, 1]^2$. This implies $r_i \le x_i \le 1-r_i$ and $r_i \le y_i \le 1-r_i$.

3.  **Optimization Method**: We use `scipy.optimize.minimize` with the SLSQP method, which handles non-linear constraints.

2.  **Optimization**: Use `scipy.optimize.minimize` with the SLSQP method. The objective is to maximize the sum of radii. We will treat radii as variables (initially equal) and centers as variables.
3.  **Constraints**:

4.  **Refinement**: Run the optimizer from a few different initial configurations (grid, hexagonal, random) to escape local minima, keeping the best result.

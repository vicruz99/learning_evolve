The strategy involves a 5x5 grid centered in the square, with the 26th circle placed in the central gap.

2.  **Constraints**:

    *   **Center Circle**: The center circle must touch the 4 surrounding grid circles to maximize its size.
3.  **Optimization**: We set up an optimization problem to maximize $25r_1 + r_2$ subject to these geometric constraints. This is solved using `scipy.optimize.minimize`.

*   Let the grid centers be $x_i = 0.5 + (i-3)\delta$ and $y_j = 0.5 + (j-3)\delta$ for $i,j \in \{1..5\}$.
*   Boundary constraint: $r_1 + 2\delta \le 1 \implies \delta \le (1-r_1)/2$.

*   Center circle constraint: Distance from center $(0.5, 0.5)$ to nearest grid center $(0.5+\delta, 0.5+\delta)$ must be $\ge r_1 + r_2$.

*   To maximize the sum, we push these constraints to equality. The tightest bound is usually the boundary constraint ($\delta = (1-r_1)/2$) combined with the center constraint.
